About SRMC

Founded in May 2022 as SRMC Credit Collection Services, we built our reputation on ethical debt recovery and professional accounts receivable management. We quickly became a trusted partner for businesses across the Philippines by prioritizing both financial results and the preservation of client relationships.

As our expertise grew, so did our horizons. We transitioned to SRMC Global Link Solutions, expanding our physical footprint to better serve our growing network.

We are more than a collection agency—we are a global bridge. By combining our roots in Collection management with high-tier customer support, we help businesses thrive through seamless, professional, and effective global links.

Why Chose SRMC

SRMC Credit Collection Services exists to provide professional and effective collection services, always striving to serve our clients in the best way possible. Our unique approach is not only what differentiates us, but also what makes us successful.

We provide a broad range of services and solutions to help organizations facilitate change, achieve their vision, and optimize performance and productivity.

Our Vision

Our vision for debt collection is to create a fair and compassionate environment where creditors and debtors can work together to resolve outstanding debts. We strive to fodter understanding and empathy, ensuring that debtors are offered reasonable options for repayment and support in overcoming financial challenges, by promoting open and respectful communication. We aim to minimize the negative impact of debt while helping individuals regain control of their financial well being

Our Mission

Our Mission in debt collection is to responsibly and ethically assist creditors in recovering outstanding debts while treating debtors with respect and dignity. We aim to facilitate positive communication, find mutually beneficial solutions and provide guidance to individuals struggling with financial obligations.
