Our System

An overview of the technology and tools that power our operations.

Vicidial Contact Center

One of the most powerful, flexible, and widely used open source contact center platforms in the world. It supports inbound, outbound, and blended operations.

Key Features:
  • Inbound, outbound & blended call handling
  • Predictive dialing & broadcast dialing
  • Integrated call recording
  • Web-based agent & admin interfaces
  • Scheduled callbacks & remote agents
  • Scalable to hundreds of seats
  • Supports standard telco lines & VoIP trunks

XLite acts as the softphone (ear & mouth) handling the audio, while Vicidial is the brain managing campaigns, lists, and dialing rules.

AI-Rudder

AI powered automation ensures we handle massive call volumes efficiently, especially during peak periods like paydays.

Core Features & Capabilities:
  • Intelligent conversation routing
  • AI powered voice agents
  • Real time agent assist
  • Analytics & insights: transcribes and analyzes 100% of calls

On spike days, AI Rudder ensures huge volumes of customer contacts and payments can be processed without delays.
