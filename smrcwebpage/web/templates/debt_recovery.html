Marketing Strategy

DEBT RECOVERY

Successful debt recovery isn’t just about being aggressive; it’s about being professional, systematic, and empathetic. The goal is to preserve the customer relationship while securing payment turning a negative situation into a demonstration of your company’s professionalism.

Firm • Fair • Professional

Key Strategies

Offer Payment Plans
  • If a good faith customer is struggling, a structured plan is better than getting nothing.
  • Focus on solutions that let customers pay rather than finding fault.
Document Everything
  • Keep detailed records of every call, email, and promise critical if a case escalates.
  • Ensure each contact and follow up drives toward a clear payment outcome.
Train Your Staff

Coach collectors to evaluate a debtor’s capacity to repay and to negotiate accordingly. Consistent training improves results and protects customer relationships.
